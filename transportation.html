03 — Transportation

Uber for Business
in Korea

Guest rides on the NESPRESSO BIFF Uber for Business group. Set up your account before arriving in Korea, then request and manage rides the same way you would with a personal Uber account.

01Before You Arrive3 02Requesting a Ride2 03During & After Your Ride3

01 · Before You Arrive in Korea

Please complete before coming to Korea
1

Log in with your personal Uber account

Sign up first if you don't already have a personal Uber account.

2

Accept the group invitation

Click the invitation link sent by email, then tap "Join Group" on Uber. Guest email addresses must be collected in advance so the invitation can be sent.

3

Confirm the group is linked

Check that NESPRESSO BIFF appears as a profile linked to your personal Uber account.

02 · Requesting a Ride

Using Uber for Business in Korea
1

Set pickup & destination as usual

Request a ride exactly as you would with a personal account — search and select your destination the normal way.

2

Check the payment method

Before confirming, make sure the payment method is set to NESPRESSO BIFF, billed to the group.

03 · During & After Your Ride

1

Meeting your driver

Share the PIN code if the driver requests it. You can message or call the driver directly through Uber — auto-translation is available for messages.

2

Receipt

A receipt is sent automatically to the group administrator after every ride — no action needed from guests.

3

After the event

Once the event concludes, the administrator removes guests from the group and access to the shared payment method ends.
